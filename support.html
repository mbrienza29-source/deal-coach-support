Deal Analysis

Deal Coach provides informational estimates and analysis based on available vehicle, financing, trade, and market data.

Results may vary depending on vehicle condition, location, lender, credit profile, market availability, and the information provided.

Independent and Consumer-Focused

Deal Coach is an independent vehicle deal analysis tool built for consumers. Deal Coach is not a dealership, lender, or vehicle marketplace and is not funded or sponsored by dealerships.

Our goal is to help consumers better understand the numbers behind their vehicle transaction so they can make their own informed decisions.
