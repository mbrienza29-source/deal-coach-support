Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 22, 2026

Overview

Deal Coach is an independent, consumer-focused vehicle deal analysis tool. This Privacy Policy explains how Deal Coach collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app and related services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Deal Coach, we may collect or process:

  • Account information, such as your email address and account identifier.
  • Vehicle information, including VIN, year, make, model, trim, mileage, and location-related vehicle data.
  • Deal information, including vehicle price, fees, taxes, financing terms, trade-in information, and other transaction details.
  • Documents and images you upload for analysis, such as dealer proposals, worksheets, purchase agreements, and contracts.
  • Information you provide about financing, including selected credit score range and loan term.
  • Subscription and entitlement information used to determine access to paid features.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the service.

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide vehicle deal analysis and Deal Score functionality.
  • Compare vehicle pricing with available market information.
  • Provide trade-in valuation information and financing benchmarks.
  • Analyze uploaded documents and contracts.
  • Compare revised deals or offers.
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts.
  • Manage subscription access and usage limits.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and improve app performance.
  • Respond to support requests.

Third-Party Services

Deal Coach uses third-party service providers to operate certain features. Information may be transmitted to these providers when necessary to provide the requested functionality.

These services may include:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and backend services.
  • OpenAI services for document and text analysis.
  • MarketCheck for vehicle market pricing information.
  • CarsXE for vehicle valuation information.
  • National Highway Traffic Safety Administration services for VIN decoding.
  • RevenueCat for subscription and entitlement management.
  • Apple for App Store purchases and subscription billing.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Uploaded Documents

Documents you upload may contain personal or financial information. You should review documents before uploading them and avoid providing information that is not necessary for Deal Coach to perform the requested analysis.

Uploaded information may be processed by Deal Coach and applicable third-party service providers solely as needed to provide app functionality, maintain the service, and comply with legal or security requirements.

Subscriptions and Payments

Purchases made through the Apple App Store are processed by Apple. Deal Coach does not receive your full payment card number from Apple.

Deal Coach may receive subscription status, product identifiers, renewal information, and related entitlement data in order to provide access to paid features.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and support legitimate business purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and how the app feature is designed.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or disclosure. No system can guarantee absolute security.
